## In short
Atemtakt collects no data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party software. We run no servers and receive no data from the app.

## What the app processes
You enter profiles in the app: a name, ventilator values (breath rate, inspiration time, target volume, pressures, PEEP), the airway, the resuscitator bag, colours, who checked the values and when, and notes. This can be health data.

This data is stored only on your device. If iCloud is turned on, Apple syncs it to your other devices through CloudKit, in your private iCloud database, under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access your private iCloud database.

For the widgets, the app keeps a short summary of each profile (name, colour, rhythm) in storage on your device that only the app and its widgets can read. Settings such as vibration and the guide's shape also stay on your device.

## Lock Screen guide
So that the guide can start on a locked iPhone without the passcode, the app hands its runnable profiles (with all their details, including notes) and your guide settings to iOS. iOS keeps them on your device; they are not transmitted. If you add the Breathing Guide control to the Lock Screen, anyone holding your iPhone can open these profiles without the passcode.

While the Lock Screen guide is open, the camera runs unseen, because iOS only allows camera extensions without the passcode. Nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted. The camera is used only if you allow access; you can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings.

## Links
The app links to these pages and to resuscitator manufacturers' documents. They open in your browser, where that provider's privacy policy applies. These pages are hosted in a public repository on GitHub, which processes technically necessary data such as your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement).

## Deleting
You can delete profiles in the app at any time; they are then also removed from iCloud. Deleting the app deletes its data on the device. You manage iCloud data in your Apple Account settings.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. As we receive none of your data, requests usually concern only the data on your device and in your iCloud account.

## Changes
If the app changes, we update this policy. The version published here applies.
